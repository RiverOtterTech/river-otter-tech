River Otter Tech is a white-label network partner for MSPs, founded by Jason Roy, a systems and network specialist who supports municipal and public-safety infrastructure.

Why River Otter Tech exists

Most MSPs have a strong help desk but no dedicated network engineer. Network tickets sit in the queue, get bounced between techs, or take up a senior engineer's whole afternoon.

River Otter Tech fills that gap. We take your switching, wireless, and firewall work, do it inside your tools under your name, and return the client to you with clean documentation.

Background

Jason's day-to-day work is supporting police, fire/EMS, and municipal networks across a multi-site Extreme Networks Fabric Connect (SPBM/IS-IS) environment. Before that, he helped manage an Aruba network through Aruba Central.

Public-safety networks don't get maintenance windows by accident. That background shapes how every client network is handled: planned changes, segmentation by default, and documentation the next engineer can use.
