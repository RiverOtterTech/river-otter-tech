Hi, I'm Jason.
I run the network side.

River Otter Tech is a white-label network engineering partner for MSPs, built on real municipal and public-safety network experience.

By day I'm a systems specialist supporting police, fire/EMS, and municipal networks across multiple sites. That network runs Extreme Networks Fabric Connect (SPBM/IS-IS), and before that I helped manage an Aruba environment through Aruba Central.

Those are networks that can't go down. Change control matters, VLAN segmentation isn't optional, and documentation has to be good enough for the next tech to pick up at 2 a.m.

Most MSPs I talk to have strong help desks but no dedicated network person. Network tickets sit, get escalated, or eat a senior tech's whole afternoon. River Otter Tech fills that gap. I work your network tickets and projects under your brand, in your tools, and hand the client back to you with clean notes.

I keep sharpening on the side, too: I run an EVE-NG lab for fabric and VOSS/EXOS work and a UniFi/FortiGate home lab.
